Our Story

A Family Table, Elevated

Apna Family Restaurant began as a small kitchen in Mirganj with one promise — to feed every guest like family. Today, that same promise lives inside a space built for celebration, comfort and honest, fresh cooking — bringing five-star hospitality to the heart of Gopalganj, Bihar.
